# Texas Labor Market Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze key trends in the **Texas labor market**.

The dashboard explores employment patterns, unemployment trends, industry performance, year-over-year growth, and long-term industry projections. It provides a clear comparison between Texas and broader U.S. labor market conditions while identifying industries with the strongest current and projected growth.

The goal of this project is to transform labor market data into meaningful and easy-to-understand insights that can support workforce planning, economic analysis, and business decision-making.

---

## Dashboard Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering questions such as:

* How has the unemployment rate in Texas changed over time?
* How does Texas unemployment compare with the overall U.S. unemployment rate?
* How has non-agricultural employment changed in Texas?
* Which industries experienced the strongest employment growth?
* Which industries experienced slower or negative growth?
* What are the fastest-growing industries in Texas?
* Which industries are projected to create the most employment opportunities through 2032?

---

## Key Dashboard Features

### Texas vs. U.S. Unemployment Analysis

The dashboard compares unemployment trends between **Texas and the United States**, allowing users to identify periods when Texas performed better or worse than the national labor market.

### Employment Trend Analysis

Historical employment data is visualized to show changes in **Texas non-agricultural employment** and overall workforce growth.

### Industry Year-over-Year Growth

Industry-level employment changes between **2024 and 2025** are analyzed to identify sectors experiencing the strongest growth.

### Industry Performance Comparison

The dashboard highlights differences in employment growth across multiple industries, making it easier to identify expanding and declining sectors.

### Long-Term Industry Projections

Employment projections from **2022–2032** are used to identify industries expected to experience strong future growth.

### Fastest-Growing Industries

The dashboard provides a visual ranking of industries with the highest projected employment growth, helping highlight potential areas of economic opportunity.

---

## Key Insights

The dashboard helps reveal several important labor market patterns:

* Texas continues to maintain a large and diverse employment base.
* Unemployment trends can be compared directly with national U.S. trends.
* Employment growth differs significantly across industries.
* Some sectors are experiencing considerably stronger year-over-year growth than others.
* Long-term projections indicate which industries are expected to contribute most significantly to future employment growth.
* Combining historical employment data with future projections provides a more complete view of the Texas labor market.
